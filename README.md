🏷️ TagShop

TagShop is a lightweight tagging and product-linking system designed to turn content into shoppable experiences. Easily attach product tags to images, videos, or posts and route users directly to purchase pages, affiliate links, or digital products.

Perfect for creators, marketers, and developers building social commerce, link-in-bio tools, or content monetisation platforms.

⸻

🚀 Features
	•	🏷️ Create and manage product tags
	•	🛒 Attach tags to content (images, videos, posts)
	•	🔗 Supports external links (Gumroad, Shopify, Amazon, custom URLs)
	•	📊 Track clicks and engagement (optional / extensible)
	•	⚡ Lightweight and fast
	•	🔌 Easy to integrate into existing projects
	•	🧩 Modular and extensible architecture
